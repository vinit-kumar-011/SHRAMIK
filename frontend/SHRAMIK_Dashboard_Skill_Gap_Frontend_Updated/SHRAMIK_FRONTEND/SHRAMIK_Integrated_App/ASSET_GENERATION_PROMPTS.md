# SHRAMIK Dashboard — Artwork Prompts

These prompts are for creating **individual reusable image assets**, not dashboard screenshots. Generate each asset separately. Ask for no labels, no user-interface components, no UI text and no brand wordmark inside the image; those belong in code.

## Global style prompt

> Create an editorial industrial illustration system for SHRAMIK, an Indian labour-market intelligence platform. Use a warm off-white paper tone, subtle halftone and etched-photo texture, black-and-white documentary imagery, and carefully restrained mustard-yellow, deep navy, orange and teal accents. The mood is civic, practical, human, trustworthy and contemporary — not futuristic, glossy, gamified or overly decorative. The art should sit naturally over a light off-white canvas with a fine dotted texture. Avoid logos, lettering, numeric labels, watermarks, UI cards, dashboard screenshots, and borders unless explicitly requested. Keep the composition legible at typical web-dashboard sizes.

## 1. Dashboard hero: worker and industrial skyline

> A single wide, panoramic editorial collage for the right side of a labour-market dashboard header. One Indian construction worker wearing a hard hat is the main subject, placed in the right half of the composition, shown from chest-up in a confident but natural pose. Behind him: fine-line cranes, scaffolding and an Indian urban construction skyline. Black-and-white documentary/halftone treatment with a large muted mustard-yellow sun disc and very small navy and teal accent shapes. Leave the left side visually quieter so the image can sit beside a large heading. Wide 2.6:1 composition, transparent background if supported, no words, no logos, no UI, no border, no watermark. Do not create a collage sheet or multiple panels; generate only this one image.

## 2. Sidebar: construction workers

> A single vertical editorial documentary illustration of two Indian construction workers seen from behind, both wearing hard hats and high-visibility workwear, looking toward cranes and an urban construction site. Black-and-white halftone/engraved-photo texture, a restrained muted-yellow sun disc and very subtle charcoal dot accents. Keep the lower and outer edges clean for use near the bottom of a narrow sidebar. Portrait 3:4 composition, transparent background if supported, no text, no logos, no UI, no border, no watermark. Do not create a contact sheet; generate only this image.

## 3. Optional wide skyline background

> A single, sparse panoramic industrial skyline with construction cranes, rebar silhouettes and a developing Indian city, rendered in fine charcoal etching and halftone on transparent background. Very low contrast around its edges so it can sit behind dashboard content; a small mustard-yellow sun and tiny muted teal accent are allowed. Wide 3:1 crop, no people in foreground, no text, no labels, no logo, no border, no UI and no watermark. Generate one image only.

## 4. Optional worker portrait set (one image per occupation)

> A single documentary-style waist-up portrait of an Indian [OCCUPATION] in authentic, practical work clothing, neutral confident expression, monochrome halftone/etched-photo treatment with a flat mustard-yellow circle behind the subject. Center the subject with a transparent background, balanced crop, no text, no logos, no UI, no border and no watermark. Generate only one portrait, not a set or contact sheet.
