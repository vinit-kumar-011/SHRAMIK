Why a connected view matters

Better workforce decisions begin with better information.

When information about demand, skills, supply and emerging requirements sits in separate places, it is hard to interpret and harder to act on. SHRAMIK brings these threads into one intelligence workflow.

The approach

One connected workflow. Five stages of intelligence.

Select a stage to learn what happens there. The descriptions explain the intended workflow; they do not imply that every pipeline step is currently operational.

01 · Gather signals

Collect relevant observations with their source references, time periods and coverage details where available.

Trust through transparency

Understand the evidence behind every insight.

Labour-market intelligence is only as useful as the evidence and assumptions behind it. SHRAMIK's approach should make source coverage, mapping quality and forecast limitations understandable.

↗

Traceable sources

Expose source references and observation periods where they are available and permitted.

≡

Transparent methods

Explain how mappings, estimates and skill-gap calculations are produced.

±

Visible uncertainty

Communicate forecast uncertainty and limitations without overstating precision.

◎

Clear provenance

Distinguish source-backed observations, derived estimates and synthetic demonstration data.

Results depend on source coverage, data quality, occupation/skill mapping choices and forecasting assumptions. Projections should be interpreted alongside supporting evidence and limitations.
